# Contacto surface

- Scope: the standalone `/contacto` enquiry route.
- Visitor mode: Persuade.
- Audience and job: Spanish and international homeowners, developers, hospitality operators, and investors who want to judge fit and begin a clear first conversation.
- Primary action: send a project enquiry. Secondary actions: call or email the studio directly.
- Proof and content: direct Málaga and Marbella presence, Costa del Sol coverage, a concise explanation of what information helps the studio respond.
- Constraints: inherit the established monochrome visual system, remain bilingual, preserve the verified phone and email details, and do not invent office addresses or commercial claims.
- Direction: a calm white opening framed as a direct conversation, followed by one rounded dark architectural plane containing the focused enquiry form.
- Memorable moment: the oversized question resolves into the form itself, making contact feel like the next project decision rather than a footer utility.
- Unresolved: connect the form to the production submission service and define server-side error states.
