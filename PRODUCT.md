# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Spanish and international homeowners, developers, hospitality operators, and investors planning high-value projects in Málaga, Marbella, and the Costa del Sol. They need an experienced multidisciplinary studio, clear guidance, technical confidence, and evidence of built quality before starting a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Fran Ruiz Arquitectos as an established Costa del Sol architecture studio, make the quality and range of its work tangible, explain its end-to-end services, and convert qualified visitors into project enquiries in Spanish or English.

## Brand Personality

Sunlit, exacting, assured. The voice is warm and direct, with the calm confidence of an experienced architect. The experience should feel modern, minimal, luxurious, light, smooth, and elegant without becoming impersonal.

## Anti-references

Generic beige-and-gold “luxury” templates, cold corporate engineering sites, resort clichés, ornamental editorial layouts, card-heavy SaaS patterns, and motion that delays access to content. The preserved original logo must never be redrawn, recolored, cropped, or distorted.

## Design Principles

1. Let the architecture lead. Project imagery carries the emotional weight.
2. Make expertise easy to understand. Translate technical depth into clear decisions and outcomes.
3. Use space as a material. Generous white space, deliberate alignment, and controlled asymmetry create calm.
4. Design for conversation. Every journey should naturally arrive at a clear, low-friction enquiry.
5. Motion reveals structure. Animation should express planes, layers, light, and sequence while respecting reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable navigation and dialogs, visible focus states, 4.5:1 minimum body-text contrast, meaningful alternative text, bilingual labels, touch targets of at least 44px, and complete reduced-motion alternatives.
